About Heyday

A salon experience shaped around care, confidence, and Black beauty culture.

Welcome to Heyday Hair Studio & Nail Bar, your beauty destination in Pretoria. Located at 817 Van der Hoff Road, Pretoria, 0082, the salon offers a welcoming space where clients can enjoy quality hair and nail services in one place.

The goal is simple: help every client look polished, feel confident, and leave feeling refreshed. From braids and protective styling to natural hair care, installs, neat men's looks and polished nails, the focus stays on detail, professionalism, and a style that suits the person wearing it.

What We Stand For

Beauty care that feels personal and professional

At Heyday, the experience is meant to feel warm, friendly, and worth coming back for. The salon aims to make self-care feel simple, comfortable, and accessible while still delivering a polished final look.

Whether a client is coming in for a fresh braided style, a touch-up, a wig install, a neat cut, or a nail appointment, the focus stays on making them feel seen, styled, and looked after.
